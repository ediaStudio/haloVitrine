We retain collected data only as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes described in this policy. Aggregated, anonymized data may be kept for longer.

Our app is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.
